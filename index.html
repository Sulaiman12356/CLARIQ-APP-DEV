Frequently Asked Questions
How is my attendance calculated? +
Each live class is 90 minutes. Your attendance % is based on how long you stayed in the session: 70–100% is Present, 20–69% is Partial, and below 20% is Absent.
When do I get my certificate? +
Certificates are issued automatically the moment you complete 100% of a course's lessons. No need to request it. You'll get a notification and can view/verify it under Certificates.
How does AI grading work on assignments? +
When you submit an assignment, it's sent to Claude for review against the assignment instructions. You'll get a grade and specific feedback, usually within a few seconds. An instructor can still manually override any grade.
When are live classes held? +
All live classes run Thursday, Friday, and Saturday, 9:00 PM – 10:30 PM. Check the Live Classes tab for the full weekly timetable and join links.
What do I do if I forget my password? +
Use "Forgot password?" on the login screen if you're logged out, or change it directly under Settings → Security if you're already logged in.

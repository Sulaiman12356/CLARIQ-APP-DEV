Meet the Founder

Onifade Sulaiman · “Mr. Clarity”

Founder & CEO, Clarity Digital Academy · Creator of CLARIQ

Closing the gap between learning and earning

Onifade Sulaiman, known to many of his students as Mr. Clarity, is the Founder & CEO of Clarity Digital Academy and the creator of CLARIQ. He built the academy after recognising that many aspiring learners struggle to find digital education that is practical, structured, and affordable enough to actually change their circumstances.

His mission is to bridge the gap between learning and earning by helping students, entrepreneurs, freelancers, and professionals acquire practical digital skills that translate directly into stronger careers and businesses. That mission is what led to CLARIQ: a platform built around structured learning paths, live mentorship, real projects, and AI-powered support, instead of another shelf of pre-recorded videos.

His work is driven by a genuine passion for digital education, technology, and artificial intelligence, with a particular focus on digital marketing and personal branding, skills he sees as among the fastest paths for young people to create real opportunity for themselves. At the centre of it all is a commitment to youth empowerment and practical learning: education that leaves you with something you can point to, not just something you remember watching.
